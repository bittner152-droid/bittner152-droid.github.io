Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy describes how shorts-factory publish (the "App"), a private tool operated by its owner, handles data received from Google APIs. The App is used only by its owner for the owner's own YouTube channels and is not offered to third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

shorts-factory publish's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Scopes and data accessed

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – to upload videos produced by the owner to the owner's own channels, including title, description, tags, privacy status and publish time.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly – to read basic information about the owner's own channels and uploaded videos (for example channel ID, video ID and processing or privacy status) in order to confirm uploads.

The App accesses only videos and metadata of the owner's own channels. It does not access data of other YouTube users, viewers or commenters.

How data is used

Data is used solely to upload the owner's videos after manual approval and to verify the result. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training of machine-learning models.

Sharing and sale

Data received from Google APIs is not shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party.

Storage and security

  • OAuth tokens are stored only as protected environment variables in the owner's private environment. They are never written to source code, files, logs or version control.
  • Upload results (such as video IDs and status) are kept in the owner's private project records only as long as needed to manage the owner's channels.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the App can no longer access the account; stored tokens become invalid and are deleted.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
